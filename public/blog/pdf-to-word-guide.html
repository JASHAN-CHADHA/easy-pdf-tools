How to Convert PDF to Word for Free

Published: June 14, 2025 · 5 min read

When You Need PDF to Word

Sometimes you receive a PDF that you need to edit – a contract, a form, or an old report. Our tool extracts the text and creates a .txt file that you can open in Word and edit freely.

Step‑by‑Step Conversion

  1. Open the PDF to Word converter.
  2. Upload your PDF (max 50 MB).
  3. Click “Convert to Word”.
  4. Download the .txt file (compatible with Word).

Note: Our converter extracts the raw text. For scanned PDFs (image‑based), text extraction is not possible – you would need OCR software.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use PDFs created from digital sources (not scans).
  • Tables and complex layouts may be simplified to plain text.
  • The output is a plain text file – you can open it with Word and reformat as needed.
