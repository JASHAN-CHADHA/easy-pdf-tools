Add Watermark to PDF Documents

Published: June 14, 2025 · 4 min read

Why Watermark PDFs?

Watermarks are essential for claiming ownership, marking drafts (“DRAFT”, “CONFIDENTIAL”), or adding a copyright notice. Our tool lets you add a text watermark to every page in seconds.

How to Add a Watermark

  1. Open the Add Watermark tool.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Enter the desired text (e.g., “COPYRIGHT”, “SAMPLE”).
  4. Click “Add Watermark”.
  5. Download the watermarked PDF.

Pro tip: Use semi‑transparent watermarks placed diagonally – they protect your content without distracting readers.

Common Use Cases

  • Legal documents – “CONFIDENTIAL”
  • Proposals – “DRAFT”
  • Portfolios – “© Your Name”
