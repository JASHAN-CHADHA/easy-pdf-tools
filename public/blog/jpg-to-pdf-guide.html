Convert JPG to PDF: Ultimate Step‑by‑Step Tutorial

Published: June 14, 2025 · 6 min read

Why Convert Images to PDF?

PDF is the standard for sharing documents because it preserves layout and works on any device. Converting JPG or PNG images to PDF lets you combine multiple photos, scans, or graphics into a single, professional document.

How to Convert Images to PDF with EasyPDF Tools

  1. Open the Image to PDF converter.
  2. Upload your images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP – up to 20 files).
  3. Choose page size (A4, Letter, or “Fit to Image”).
  4. Select orientation (portrait / landscape) and quality.
  5. Click “Convert to PDF”.
  6. Download your PDF – each image becomes a separate page.

Pro tip: To create a PDF with one image per page, simply upload images in the order you want them to appear.

Supported Formats & Limits

  • ✅ JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP
  • ✅ Up to 20 images per conversion
  • ✅ Max 50 MB per image
