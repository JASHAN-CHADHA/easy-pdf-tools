Published: June 14, 2025 · 8 min read

Save Time and Money with These Tools

As a student, you constantly handle PDFs – lecture slides, research papers, assignments, and forms. Instead of paying for expensive software, use these 100% free tools from EasyPDF Tools.

1. Merge PDF – Combine Lecture Notes

Instead of having 10 separate PDF files for one subject, merge them into a single document. Ideal for exam revision.

2. Image to PDF – Scan and Submit

Convert photos of handwritten homework or whiteboard notes into a clean PDF.

3. Compress PDF – Stay Within Upload Limits

Many university portals limit file sizes. Compress your PDF to meet the requirement without losing quality.

4. Word to PDF – Submit Professional Assignments

Convert your essays from .docx to PDF so that formatting remains intact for the professor.

5. PDF to Word – Extract Text from Scanned Books

Turn a PDF textbook chapter into editable text so you can quote, highlight, and take notes.
