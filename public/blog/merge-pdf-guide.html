How to Merge PDF Files: Complete Guide for Beginners

Published: June 14, 2025 · 7 min read

Why Merge PDFs?

Combining multiple PDF files into one document is a common need – whether you're merging invoices, compiling research papers, or creating a single portfolio. With EasyPDF Tools you can do it in seconds, for free, without installing any software.

Step‑by‑Step Tutorial

  1. Go to our Merge PDF tool.
  2. Click “Browse Files” or drag & drop your PDFs (up to 10 files).
  3. Arrange the files in the order you want them merged.
  4. Click “Merge PDFs”.
  5. Download your merged document instantly – it's that easy.

Pro tip: The merger preserves all original formatting, bookmarks, and links. Files are automatically deleted from our servers after 1 hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free?

Yes – unlimited merges, no sign‑up, no watermarks.

Can I merge different file types?

Only PDF files can be merged directly. However, you can first convert images or Word documents to PDF using our other tools, then merge them.
