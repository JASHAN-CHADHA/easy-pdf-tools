How to Compress PDF Files Without Losing Quality

Published: June 14, 2025 · 5 min read

When Should You Compress a PDF?

Large PDFs are hard to email, slow to upload, and consume storage. Compressing them solves these issues. Our tool optimizes the file structure and compresses images while keeping text crisp.

Step‑by‑Step Compression

  1. Visit the Compress PDF tool.
  2. Upload your PDF (max 50 MB).
  3. Click “Compress PDF”.
  4. Download the smaller, optimised version.

Pro tip: Our compression is lossless for text and uses smart image optimisation. You can compress a file multiple times, but the first pass usually gives the best result.

How Much Can You Compress?

  • Text‑heavy PDFs: 10‑20% reduction
  • Scanned documents: 30‑60% reduction
  • Image‑rich PDFs: 40‑70% reduction
