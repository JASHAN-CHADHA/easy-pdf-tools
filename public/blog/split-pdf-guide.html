How to Split PDF Files: Extract Specific Pages

Published: June 14, 2025 · 5 min read

When to Split a PDF

You might need only a few pages from a large PDF – for example, a single chapter from an ebook, a specific invoice from a batch, or a signature page from a contract. Our split tool gives you exactly the pages you want.

Step‑by‑Step Split

  1. Go to the Split PDF tool.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Enter the page numbers or range (e.g., 1-5 or 1,3,7-9).
  4. Click “Split PDF”.
  5. Download the new document containing only the selected pages.

Pro tip: You can extract multiple non‑consecutive ranges: 2-4,7,10-12.

Format Examples

  • 5 – extracts only page 5
  • 2-8 – extracts pages 2 through 8
  • 1,3,5 – extracts pages 1, 3 and 5
  • 1-3,8,10-15 – extracts pages 1‑3, page 8, and pages 10‑15
